Wire Copy

Add Wire Copy to your browser

  1. 1

    Open the extensions page

    Copy this address, then paste it into the address bar of your browser and press Enter. It works in Chrome, Chromium, Vivaldi, Brave and Edge.

    chrome://extensions
  2. 2

    Turn on Developer mode

    It is the switch at the top-right of the extensions page.

  3. 3

    Load the extension folder

    Click Load unpacked and choose this folder:

    the WireCopy-Extension folder

    Or drag the WireCopy-Extension folder (Wire Copy opened it for you) onto the extensions page.
